01 / About

An engineer who likes to see the whole system work.

I'm Sithum, an Electrical and Information Engineering undergraduate at the University of Ruhuna, from Kandy, Sri Lanka. My foundation is in digital design, embedded systems and software development, and I'm most at home with Verilog, FPGA-based design and custom PCBs.

My projects run from multi-UAV control and real-time telemetry to IoT platforms and embedded firmware. I trained at the National Water Supply and Drainage Board's Kandy South Water Treatment Plant, and I'm now a trainee telecommunications engineer at Sri Lanka Telecom. Away from the bench I play rated chess and swim competitively.

04 / Projects

Things I've built.

Electronics · Telemetry · Featured

AeroSync 5: Multi-Drone Synchronization System

A ground-controlled system for real-time synchronized flight of five quadcopters. I built a centralized Ground Control Station for multi-drone command, an ESP32-based low-latency telemetry network, and formation control for line, V-shape and circular flight patterns.

  • ESP32
  • CC3D Revo Mini
  • Distributed control
  • Wireless telemetry
  • Formation control
LinkedIn post

Electronics

Crazyflie Nano Quadcopter: Custom Rebuild & Firmware

Rebuilt the Bitcraze Crazyflie micro quadcopter on a custom PCB with an ESP32-S3 and MPU6050 IMU, wrote firmware to use the ESP32's Wi-Fi, and tuned the PID loops through systematic flight testing.

  • ESP32-S3
  • MPU6050
  • Custom PCB
  • PID tuning
LinkedIn post

Digital design · FPGA

FPGA Reaction Time Tester

A reaction-time measurement system on a Nexys A7-100T in SystemVerilog: an FSM controller, an LFSR random delay to prevent anticipation, a 1 ms tick from the 100 MHz clock, and false-start and overflow detection on a multiplexed 7-segment display.

  • SystemVerilog
  • FSM design
  • LFSR
  • Nexys A7

Electronics · Control

Two-Wheel Self-Balancing Robot

A self-balancing robot using an MPU6050 IMU, a Kalman filter and a tuned PID controller. Bluetooth gives live parameter logging, and a separate circuit allows manual PID tuning.

  • C++
  • Kalman filter
  • PID control
  • Bluetooth

IoT · Networking

Water Turbidity & Level Monitoring Systems

Two NodeMCU-based IoT systems for water monitoring: a web platform tracking reservoir levels, flow rates and water quality in real time, and a portable turbidity meter that logs readings to a Google Sheet over Wi-Fi.

  • NodeMCU
  • IoT
  • Web platform
  • Google Sheets API

IoT · Healthcare

Health and Posture Monitoring System

A wearable for elderly people living alone. An IMU tracks posture and detects falls, a pulse oximeter monitors heart rate and SpO2, and data goes over MQTT to the cloud and an Android app, with GSM SMS alerts.

  • ESP32
  • MQTT
  • GSM
  • Pulse oximeter
  • Android app

SkillShare: Learning Management Website
